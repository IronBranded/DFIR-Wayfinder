# Sources & Attribution

This academy is original writing built from the facts, structures, and taxonomies published by the following:

- **SANS** — course material and posters (FOR500, FOR508, FOR572, and the certification program generally)
- **Microsoft** — Microsoft Learn documentation, Microsoft Internals research, Microsoft Cloud/Entra documentation, and MSTIC (Microsoft Threat Intelligence Center) publications
- **13cubed** (Richard Davis) — NTFS timestamp and Prefetch research specifically cited in Level 2
- **MITRE ATT&CK** — the technique taxonomy used throughout

Nothing is reproduced verbatim from any source; each lesson links out to primaries for readers who want the original material. This academy is built for enterprise DFIR — SOC analysts, incident responders, and threat hunters — not for criminal-prosecution workflows.

## A note on how this academy was built

The lesson/level structure and interactive engine (progress tracking, quizzes, search) are adapted from IronBranded's own [Malware Analysis Academy](https://ironbranded.github.io/Malware-Analysis-Academy/), reused with a new visual identity and re-themed for DFIR content specifically. The underlying DFIR content itself is this project's own knowledge base, restructured and re-authored lesson by lesson from its earlier, MkDocs-based form into this interactive format.
